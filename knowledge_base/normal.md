# Normal Chest X-ray

## Overview
A normal chest X-ray means the visible lung fields do not show obvious signs of pneumonia or tuberculosis on the image. A normal image does not rule out every disease, especially when symptoms are early, mild, or caused by a condition that is not visible on plain X-ray.

## Clinical Context
Clinicians interpret a normal chest X-ray together with symptoms, oxygen level, medical history, examination findings, and sometimes laboratory tests. If cough, fever, chest pain, weight loss, or breathing difficulty continues, additional testing may still be needed.

## Next Steps
For a normal prediction, the result should be treated as reassuring but not final. Persistent or worsening symptoms should be reviewed by a qualified clinician.
