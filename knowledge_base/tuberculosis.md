# Tuberculosis

## Overview
Tuberculosis is an infection caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Chest X-ray findings can suggest TB, but diagnosis usually requires clinical evaluation and microbiological testing rather than imaging alone.

## Symptoms
Symptoms can include a long-lasting cough, fever, night sweats, weight loss, fatigue, chest pain, and sometimes coughing blood. Symptoms and imaging findings can vary depending on whether disease is active or latent.

## Next Steps
When TB is suspected, clinicians may request sputum smear, culture, nucleic acid amplification testing, drug-susceptibility testing, or additional imaging. Prompt follow-up matters because untreated active TB can spread and can require specific multi-drug treatment.
