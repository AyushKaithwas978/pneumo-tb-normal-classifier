# Pneumonia

## Overview
Pneumonia is an infection that can inflame the air sacs in one or both lungs. On chest X-ray, pneumonia may appear as areas of increased opacity, consolidation, or patchy infiltrates, but imaging findings must be interpreted with symptoms and clinical examination.

## Symptoms
Common symptoms can include cough, fever, chills, chest discomfort, fatigue, and difficulty breathing. Some patients, especially older adults or people with other illnesses, may have less typical symptoms.

## Next Steps
A clinician may consider oxygen measurement, blood tests, sputum testing, repeat imaging, or treatment depending on severity and clinical context. Urgent care is important for severe shortness of breath, confusion, chest pain, blue lips, or low oxygen levels.
